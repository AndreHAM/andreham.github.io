Research

Storing useful energy, controlling heat, and understanding interacting quantum systems.

I develop theoretical approaches to energy storage and heat control in open quantum systems, connecting quantum information with thermodynamic device design.

My work combines analytical models and numerical analysis to identify useful physical mechanisms, their limitations, and routes towards experimentally testable implementations.

Current direction · SONATINA 2025–2028

Modular quantum thermal devices

I lead a project at ICTQT, University of Gdańsk, investigating how heat manipulation and energy storage can be integrated in multifunctional quantum devices. A central goal is to adapt theoretical designs to superconducting circuits.

01 · Quantum energy storage

Charging and protecting useful energy

I study how measurements, controlled operations and engineered couplings affect the energy that can be extracted from a quantum battery.

Our contributions include charge-preserving operations, protocols that mitigate environmental discharge, and a charging scheme based on chiral squeezing.

  • Chiral squeezing PRL · 2026
  • Charge-preserving operations PRX Energy · 2026
  • Ergotropy protection PRX Energy · 2025

Energy storage in an open quantum battery.

02 · Quantum thermal devices

Controlling heat flow

I investigate how interactions and energy-level structures determine thermal functionality. We showed how detuning in a three-subsystem model can support heat switching, rectification, stabilization and amplification.

  • Detuning effects for heat-current control Physical Review E · 2024

A quantum counterpart of a thermal transistor.

03 · Thermodynamic foundations

Local energy in interacting systems

We proposed a Schmidt-decomposition framework for the energetics of autonomous bipartite quantum systems. It identifies local effective Hamiltonians and preserves energy additivity, including in strong-coupling scenarios.

  • A Schmidt decomposition approach Entropy · 2022
  • On the energetic analysis of autonomous quantum systems PhD thesis · 2022

Local effective Hamiltonians and energy additivity.
